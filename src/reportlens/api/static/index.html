    How it works
    
    ReportLens answers questions about the annual reports of RBC, Scotiabank and
      BCE for fiscal 2021–2024: 12 reports split into about 6,000 passages, plus the
      headline figures pulled out of their financial tables and a knowledge graph of segments,
      subsidiaries and executives. It's a portfolio project that puts several retrieval (RAG)
      techniques side by side, with an evaluation suite measuring what each one adds.
